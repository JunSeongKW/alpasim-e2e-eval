\section{Controller settings}
\label{app:mpc}

The selected trajectory is tracked in closed loop by AlpaSim's MPC controller.
Controller gains are a submitted artefact rather than a fixed part of the
evaluation contract; all values below lie inside the published bounds, $[0,10]$
for the six weights and integer $[0,19]$ for \texttt{idx\_start\_penalty}.

\begin{table}[h]
\centering
\small
\begin{tabular}{lcc}
\toprule
Setting & AlpaSim default & \textbf{Ours} \\
\midrule
\texttt{mpc\_implementation}              & \texttt{linear} & \textbf{\texttt{nonlinear}} \\
\texttt{n\_horizon}                       & 20   & 20 \\
\texttt{dt\_mpc}                          & 0.1  & 0.1 \\
\midrule
\texttt{long\_position\_weight}           & 2.0  & \textbf{0.5} \\
\texttt{lat\_position\_weight}            & 1.0  & \textbf{6.0} \\
\texttt{idx\_start\_penalty}              & 10   & \textbf{3} \\
\texttt{heading\_weight}                  & 1.0  & 1.0 \\
\texttt{acceleration\_weight}             & 0.1  & 0.1 \\
\texttt{rel\_front\_steering\_angle\_weight} & 5.0 & 5.0 \\
\texttt{rel\_acceleration\_weight}        & 1.0  & 1.0 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\caption{MPC controller configuration. Four settings differ from the AlpaSim
defaults; the remaining four are unchanged.}
\label{tab:mpc}
\end{table}

Equivalently, as Hydra overrides on top of the \texttt{nonlinear} controller
preset:

\begin{verbatim}
controller=nonlinear
controller.gains.long_position_weight=0.5
controller.gains.lat_position_weight=6.0
controller.gains.idx_start_penalty=3
\end{verbatim}
